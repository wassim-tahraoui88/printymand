From the editor's desk

T unisia has been printing for centuries — on cotton, on tile, on paper pulled from olive press cake. What it has never had, until now, is a place where the designer in Sfax meets the pressroom in Tunis meets the customer in Sousse without an intermediary or a foreign currency in between.

Printymand is that place. End-to-end, 100% local — payments in dinars, delivery in days, royalties paid the week a thing sells. The platform is the connective tissue; the craft belongs to the people who make it.

IV.
How a Printymand is made
Step 01 I

Browse the plates

Pick a design from Tunisian artists. Every work is printable on shirts, hoodies, totes and mugs.

Step 02 II

Customize & choose a pressroom

Set product, colour, size and quantity, then pick a verified local pressroom and send the request.

Step 03 III

Accept, pay & press

The pressroom accepts, you pay in dinars, and your order is queued and quality-checked at the shop you chose.

Step 04 IV

Wear it. Repeat.

Delivered in two to four days inside Tunisia. Free returns for fourteen days.
