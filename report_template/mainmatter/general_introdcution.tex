\chapter*{General Introduction}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{General Introduction}
In today's digital age, the demand for efficient data processing and management has become critical across various industries. Businesses and individuals alike require systems that can swiftly convert physical documents into digital formats, thereby saving time and reducing errors. Optical Character Recognition (OCR)technology plays a critical role in this transformation by enabling the automatic extraction of text from images and documents.

Manual data entry from physical documents is time-consuming and prone to errors. Organizations often struggle with organizing and extracting relevant information from large volumes of documents, leading to inefficiencies and potential inaccuracies. This problem necessitates a solution that automates the extraction, verification, and organization of data from documents..\\

\vspace{1mm}

In this context, and as part of our project for the End-of-Studies project conducted within the company Elite Council Consulting to develop and design an advanced OCR application. Our solution automates the extraction and organization of data from various document types, integrates verification processes, and includes functionalities for exporting data in multiple formats.
To better organize our work, we have structured it as follows .
 \begin{itemize}
     \item The first chapter "\textbf{ Preliminary Study}" introduces the project and provides an overview of the scope and objectives. It includes a presentation of the host organization, a study of existing OCR solutions, and a detailed look at the problem we aim to solve, followed by our proposed solution and methodology.
 \end{itemize}
 \begin{itemize}
     \item The second chapter "\textbf{Architecture and Planning}" delves into the technical requirements and design. It discusses the application's architecture, sprint planning, and the work environment, laying the groundwork for transforming raw images into structured data.
 \end{itemize}
 \begin{itemize}
     \item The third chapter "\textbf{Sprint 1: Image Uploading, Preprocessing, and Scanning}" covers the initial development phase, focusing on image upload, preprocessing, and OCR scanning. This chapter details the sprint backlog, design phase and development efforts.
     \end{itemize}
\begin{itemize}
    \item The fourth chapter "\textbf{Sprint 2: Data Structuring, Verification, Storing, and Transforming}" discusses the second sprint, focusing on data structuring, verification, and transformation. It presents the sprint backlog, design diagrams, and development progress.
\end{itemize}
\begin{itemize}
    \item The fifth and final chapter "\textbf{Sprint 3: Data Export and System Enhancement}" explores the final sprint, highlighting the implementation of data export functionalities and system enhancements. This chapter reviews the sprint backlog, developed functionalities and design efforts, culminating in the completion of the project.
\end{itemize}

Finally, we concluded the report with a general conclusion, where we discussed the valuable experience gained, the practical benefits of our application, and the insights into project management and teamwork. We highlighted the impact of well-designed technology on improving daily tasks and productivity, and expressed our gratitude for the support received throughout this journey.
