================================================================================
1. THE CORE IDEA
================================================================================

Printymand is a Tunisian print-on-demand MARKETPLACE. Nothing is manufactured
in advance; an item is printed only after it is ordered. No inventory exists
anywhere in the system — there is no stock field, no warehouse, no quantity on
hand.

It is a THREE-SIDED MARKETPLACE plus a supervisor:

    DESIGNERS  publish artwork and earn a fixed royalty on every unit sold.
    PRINTERS   ("pressrooms") produce and ship the physical item locally.
    CUSTOMERS  buy a design, printed on a product they pick, by a printer
               they pick.
    ADMIN      owns the product catalog and the pricing policy, verifies
               accounts, moderates artwork, and governs the platform.

The platform never prints anything and never owns stock. It owns exactly three
things:

    1. THE CATALOG      what can be printed on (global, admin-owned)
    2. THE PRICING      the margin and the royalty (two numbers, global)
    3. TRUST            verification, moderation, ranks, reviews

Revenue is a fixed margin added to every unit of every order.

WHY IT IS BUILT THIS WAY (visible in the structure of the code):
Because the catalog is global and the printers merely opt into it, printers
are interchangeable and directly comparable on price, speed and rank. A
designer publishes once and instantly becomes sellable through every printer
that offers that product. Capacity scales by recruiting another print shop —
not by buying another machine. That is the whole business model in one
sentence.


================================================================================
2. THE MONEY
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 THE PRICE FORMULA — THERE IS ONLY ONE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    UNIT PRICE = PRINTER'S OFFERING PRICE + PLATFORM MARGIN
    LINE TOTAL = (printer's offering price + platform margin) x quantity

Nothing else enters the price. No percentage commission exists anywhere in
the system.

Platform settings (admin-editable at runtime, DEFAULT_PLATFORM_SETTINGS):

    margin           = 10 TND    fixed, added per unit
    designerRoyalty  =  5 TND    fixed, earned per unit sold
    payoutThreshold  = 50 TND    minimum designer balance to cash out
    categories       = Culture, Typography, Minimal, Nature, Streetwear, Retro

Worked example (the one written in the code comments):
        printer prices a t-shirt at   30 TND
      + platform margin                10 TND
      = customer pays                  40 TND

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.2 THE SPLIT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Every order line freezes three numbers at the moment it is created:

    printerAmount   = printer's offering price x quantity   -> printer
    platformFee     = margin x quantity                     -> platform
    designerRoyalty = royalty x quantity                    -> designer

THE ROYALTY IS FUNDED OUT OF THE MARGIN. It is not added on top of the price.
With the defaults:

    Customer pays      printer price + 10
    Printer receives   printer price
    Designer receives  5
    Platform NET       10 - 5 = 5

For CUSTOMER-UPLOADED artwork, designerRoyalty = 0 and the platform keeps the
whole 10.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.3 THE THREE PRICING LAWS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

LAW 1 — THE DESIGNER HAS ZERO PRICING POWER.
    There is no price field anywhere in the designer's upload wizard. Every
    designer earns the same flat royalty per unit, on every design. The legacy
    `Design.price` field still exists in the model but is explicitly NOT part
    of what a customer pays — it is retained only for historical display.

LAW 2 — THE ADMIN SETS A FLOOR, THE PRINTER PRICES ABOVE IT.
    Each global product carries `basePrice`, which is the MINIMUM any printer
    may charge for it. Saving an offering below the floor silently raises it
    and returns a message to the printer:
        "Price raised to the <floor> TND minimum set by the platform."
    Above the floor, printers compete freely.

LAW 3 — THE MARGIN IS FIXED AND GLOBAL.
    One number, applied to every unit of every line of every order. Changing
    it in admin settings reprices the entire marketplace instantly.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.4 DESIGNER PAYOUTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Royalties ACCRUE to `designerProfile.payoutBalance` only when an order is
    fully settled (section 5.4) — not when it is placed, and not when it is
    paid.
  - A payout can only be requested when balance >= payoutThreshold:
        "You need at least 50 TND to request a payout."
  - Requesting takes the ENTIRE balance, zeroes it, and creates a PayoutRecord
    with status 'requested' and a due date 7 days out (PAYOUT_TERM_DAYS = 7).
    Reference format: PO-<last 6 digits of the timestamp>.
  - UI confirms: "Payout requested. It will be processed within 7 days."
  - Payout statuses: scheduled | requested | processing | paid

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.5 PRINTER PAYOUTS — DELIBERATELY ASYMMETRIC
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - A PrinterPayoutRecord is created automatically per order line the moment
    the order becomes fully Delivered, for that line's `printerAmount`.
  - It is created with status 'paid' immediately. There is NO threshold, NO
    request step and NO waiting period for printers.
  - The pressroom's `revenue` counter increases by the same amount.

So: printers are settled automatically per delivered order; designers must
accumulate to a threshold and then request. This asymmetry is intentional in
the code.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.6 CURRENCY AND PAYMENT METHODS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Currency is TND everywhere. There is no multi-currency support anywhere.

Payment methods in the model:
    'd17'     D17 mobile wallet     <- default selected at checkout
    'paymee'  Paymee gateway
    'card'    bank card
    'cash'    cash on delivery

No payment integration exists in the frontend. Paying flips paymentStatus to
'paid' and advances the lines. A real gateway is a backend concern.

3. THE FOUR ROLES
================================================================================

There are exactly four roles in the system:

    UserRole = 'customer' | 'designer' | 'printer' | 'admin'

VISITOR is not a role — it is simply an unauthenticated session, which can
reach the public routes listed in 3.2.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 WHAT EACH ROLE IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

CUSTOMER
    Browses, customizes, picks a printer, requests, pays, tracks, reviews.
    Can also upload their OWN artwork for private personal printing.
    ACTIVE immediately on registration — no verification required.

DESIGNER
    Publishes artwork, assigns it to products, configures how it sits on each
    one, runs a public storefront, watches analytics, requests payouts.
    Must be VERIFIED BY AN ADMIN before gaining full access.
    A designer is ALSO a buyer — they are included in BUYER_ROLES.

PRINTER (pressroom)
    Opts into global products, prices them, sets availability, accepts or
    rejects incoming requests, then drives production forward.
    Must be VERIFIED BY AN ADMIN before gaining full access.
    A PRINTER CANNOT BUY. Stated in the router as: "Printers fulfil orders;
    they do not buy."

ADMIN
    Owns the catalog, the pricing policy and the category list. Verifies,
    suspends, bans and re-roles accounts. Moderates designs. Features content.
    Sees platform-wide revenue. Can also buy, and can view any design in any
    state.

4. THE CATALOG MODEL — THE STRUCTURAL HEART
================================================================================

Three separate things, owned by three different parties. Getting this right is
what makes Printymand a marketplace rather than a print shop.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 PRODUCT — global, owned by the ADMIN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

There is exactly ONE "T-Shirt", ONE "Mug", ONE "Phone Case" on the entire
platform. Products are the PLATFORM's catalog, never a printer's catalog.

Fields: name, category, description, basePrice (THE FLOOR), colors[], sizes[],
mockup images[], availability (ACTIVE | PAUSED | DRAFT), leadTimeDays, rating,
totalOrders.

ONLY THE ADMIN creates, edits or deletes products. Printers cannot create
products or upload mockups. Designers cannot either.

Seeded catalog (all owned by "Printymand", printerId 0):

    ID   NAME         CATEGORY     FLOOR   COLOURS                  SIZES
    601  T-Shirt      Apparel      28 TND  white/black/navy/forest  S..XXL
    602  Hoodie       Apparel      52 TND  black/sand/burgundy      M..XXL
    603  Mug          Drinkware    21 TND  white/black              11oz
    604  Tote Bag     Accessories  24 TND  sand/black               One Size
    605  Phone Case   Accessories  29 TND  black/white              iPhone/Samsung
    606  Sticker      Accessories   8 TND  white                    S/M/L

Admin product categories: Apparel, Drinkware, Accessories, Home.
(Note: these PRODUCT categories are a hardcoded list in the admin dashboard
and are a different list from the DESIGN categories in platform settings.)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.2 PRINTER PRODUCT OFFERING — owned by the PRINTER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A printer OPTS IN to a global product and attaches:
    - their own production price (clamped to >= the product floor)
    - their own description for that product
    - an available on/off toggle

Several printers offer the SAME global product. That is the competition. There
is still only one "Phone Case" — printers just compete on it.

Seeded offerings, showing the competition on the T-Shirt (floor 28):
    PrintPro Tunisia      28 TND
    Atelier Couleurs      26 TND    <- seeded below the current floor
    Studio Press Nabeul   27 TND

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.3 DESIGN — owned by a DESIGNER, or by a CUSTOMER for private uploads
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Fields: title, image, categories[], tags[] (max 10), description, rating,
sales, views, engagementRate, conversionRate, status, moderation, nsfw flag,
customizationAllowed, isUserUpload, uploadedByUserId, assignedProductIds[],
productConfigurations[].

    DesignStatus     = ACTIVE | ARCHIVED | REMOVED
    ModerationStatus = PENDING | APPROVED | REJECTED

THE DESIGN<->PRODUCT LINK IS STORED ON ONE SIDE ONLY.
`design.assignedProductIds` is the only stored direction. The reverse view
("which designs fit this mug?") is DERIVED at read time by
availableDesignsForProduct(). The two directions therefore cannot contradict
each other. Preserve this if you build a backend — do not add a reverse
column.

Per assigned product, the designer stores a DesignProductConfiguration:
    defaultPlacement { x, y, scale }   where the art sits on the mockup
                                       (DEFAULT_PLACEMENT = x 50, y 48,
                                        scale 0.42 — centred, slightly high,
                                        42% of width)
    availableColors[]                  a SUBSET of the product's colours
    availableSizes[]                   a SUBSET of the product's sizes
    title, description                 optional per-product copy (a mug blurb
                                       differs from a t-shirt blurb)

5. THE ORDER LIFECYCLE — THE MOST IMPORTANT MECHANIC
================================================================================

Printymand uses an APPROVAL-GATED order flow:

        NO MONEY IS TAKEN UNTIL A PRINTER HAS ACCEPTED THE JOB.

A customer does not "buy". They send a REQUEST. A printer accepts or rejects.
Only after acceptance can the customer pay. This protects the buyer from
paying for work nobody can do, and the printer from being committed to a job
they cannot deliver.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 THE STATE MACHINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Order-level (OrderRequestStatus):
    REQUESTED -> ACCEPTED -> (payment) -> fulfilment
    REQUESTED -> REJECTED           printer refused
    REQUESTED -> CANCELLED          customer withdrew
    ACCEPTED  -> CANCELLED          customer withdrew before paying

Payment (PaymentStatus):   unpaid | pending | processing | paid

Line-level (OrderLineStatus):
    Pending -> Confirmed -> Printing -> Shipped -> Delivered
    Pending -> Rejected

Customer-facing stage, DERIVED by getOrderStatus() — never stored:
    Rejected            requestStatus REJECTED
    Cancelled           requestStatus CANCELLED
    Requested           requestStatus REQUESTED
    Awaiting payment    accepted but not paid
    Delivered           ALL lines Delivered
    Shipped             ANY line Shipped
    Printing            ANY line Printing
    Confirmed           paid, nothing started

    In flight  = Requested, Awaiting payment, Confirmed, Printing, Shipped
    Terminal   = Delivered, Rejected, Cancelled

Tracking timeline shown to the customer — six steps:
    Requested -> Accepted -> Paid -> Printing -> Shipped -> Delivered
For a rejected or cancelled order, only "Requested" is marked done.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.2 STEP BY STEP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 1 — REQUEST (customer, from printer selection)
    One order is created containing ONE LINE PER PRODUCT — a buyer can order
    the same design on a t-shirt AND a mug in a single request. All lines go
    to ONE chosen printer. Created REQUESTED / unpaid, with all three money
    figures frozen onto each line.
    Order id and tracking code are the same value: PMD-YYMMDD-<sequence>
    e.g. PMD-260330-801. (One canonical reference, so the two cannot drift.)
    -> printer notified:  "New order request <id> is awaiting your decision."
    -> customer notified: "Request <id> sent to <printer>. Waiting for
                           acceptance."

STEP 2 — DECISION (printer)
    ACCEPT -> requestStatus ACCEPTED
              "Your request <id> was accepted. Go to your cart to pay."
    REJECT -> requestStatus REJECTED, EVERY line set to Rejected, reason
              recorded (default: "The printer is unable to fulfill this
              request.")
    Answered once only. A second attempt returns:
              "This request has already been processed."

STEP 3 — CANCEL (customer, optional)
    Allowed while REQUESTED, and still allowed once ACCEPTED as long as the
    order is unpaid. A paid order cannot be cancelled here:
        "A paid order can no longer be cancelled here."
    All lines are set to Rejected and the reason becomes "Cancelled by the
    customer."
    -> printer notified: "Order <id> was canceled by the customer."

STEP 4 — PAYMENT (customer, from /cart or from the tracking page)
    Possible ONLY when requestStatus is ACCEPTED:
        "You can only pay once the printer has accepted your request."
    On success: paymentStatus = 'paid' and every Pending line -> Confirmed.
    Shipping address and payment method can still be edited at this moment.
    -> printer notified:  "Order <id> is paid — start fulfillment."
    -> customer notified: "Payment received for order <id>. It is now
                           confirmed."

STEP 5 — FULFILMENT (printer)
    Each line advances ONE STEP AT A TIME:
        Confirmed -> Printing -> Shipped -> Delivered
    A Rejected line can never be advanced. The customer is notified on every
    single transition.

STEP 6 — SETTLEMENT (automatic) — see 5.4

STEP 7 — REVIEW (customer) — see section 8

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.3 THE "CART" IS NOT A SHOPPING CART
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Unusual and deliberate. /cart and /checkout render the SAME page, and it does
NOT list things you are about to buy. It lists ORDERS A PRINTER HAS ALREADY
ACCEPTED AND THAT YOU HAVE NOT PAID FOR (awaitingPaymentOrdersForUser).

"Removing" an entry from this cart CANCELS the underlying order request and
notifies the printer.

LEGACY PATH: a classic cart still exists in the store (cartItems,
submitOrderRequest, finalizeLocalOrder) but no live screen adds to it. Be
aware that its pricing helper `cartForUser()` computes
`product.basePrice + margin` — it uses the PRODUCT FLOOR, ignores the chosen
printer's offering price, and ignores quantity entirely. It disagrees with
the real order-creation path. Treat the printer-selection path as canonical
and this one as dead code.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.4 SETTLEMENT — WHEN VALUE ACTUALLY MOVES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

An order settles ONCE, automatically, the moment EVERY line reaches Delivered
(and the order is ACCEPTED and paid). It is idempotent: an order that already
produced printer payouts is never settled again.

On settlement:
    1. Each line's designerRoyalty is credited to that design's designer
       `payoutBalance`. Skipped entirely for customer-uploaded artwork.
    2. A PrinterPayoutRecord (status 'paid') is created per line for the
       printer's `printerAmount`; the pressroom's revenue counter grows.
    3. The printer's fulfillmentScore += 5, and their rank is recomputed.
    4. The designer's salesScore increases, and their rank is recomputed.
    5. Each credited designer is notified:
       "You earned <amount> TND in royalties from order <id>."

TWO THINGS TO FIX WHEN YOU REBUILD THIS SERVER-SIDE:

    (a) The designer salesScore increment is (number of distinct designers
        credited on the order + 1) — NOT the number of units sold. For a
        normal single-designer order that is always +2, whatever the
        quantity. This looks like an oversight rather than a rule.

    (b) Rating aggregation blends as (old + new) / 2, rounded to 1 decimal.
        That is a running blend, not a mean — the newest review always
        carries 50% of the weight. A real average needs a stored count.

6. WORKFLOW — VISITOR (unauthenticated)
================================================================================

HOME (/)
    Editorial "issue" layout. Shows:
      - 4 featured designs, sourced from the marketplace listing so private
        uploads and unmoderated work can NEVER surface on the public cover
      - 3 ACTIVE products
      - "mood tiles" — the platform category list, each with a presentation
        label and icon (Culture 🏛️, Typography -> "Calligraphy" ✒️,
        Minimal ◻️, Nature 🍃, Streetwear 👕, Retro 📷)
      - a maker spotlight and a directory of non-retired pressrooms
      - PUBLIC STATS ONLY: designer count, active design count, pressroom
        count, delivered order count.
        Gross revenue and total user count are DELIBERATELY EXCLUDED — those
        live in the admin-only overview.
      - the main CTA switches: "Create your account" when signed out,
        "Open workspace" when signed in.

MARKETPLACE (/marketplace)
    Lists ONLY admin-APPROVED, ACTIVE designs. Private customer uploads never
    appear.
    Filters: free-text search, category pill, and optionally a product.
             Search matches title, designer name, description, every category
             and every tag.
    Sorts:   Trending (default) = views + sales x 8
             Newest       by createdAt
             Top rated    by rating
             Price low->high / high->low, computed on WHAT THE BUYER ACTUALLY
             PAYS (cheapest assigned product floor + margin), not the legacy
             design fee. Designs with no printable product sort last.
    Filters are mirrored into the URL query string, so a filtered marketplace
    is shareable and survives a reload. An active-filter count badge drives a
    clear-all action.

PRODUCTS (/products)
    The global catalog. DRAFT products are hidden. Filter by category, by
    pressroom (i.e. "show me only what this printer can make"), and search.
    Sort by popularity (totalOrders), rating, or price.

DESIGN DETAIL (/design/:id)
    Visibility rules (canView):
        - admin sees everything
        - a private upload is visible only to the customer who uploaded it
        - a designer always sees their own work, in any state
        - everyone else needs status ACTIVE and moderation APPROVED
    A bad or forbidden id renders "not found" — it NEVER silently falls back
    to another design.

STOREFRONT (/storefront/:id)
    Public designer shop: banner, avatar, name, rank badge, bio, design count,
    total sales, level, portfolio links, design grid. Public visitors see only
    ACTIVE + APPROVED designs.

Any attempt to order redirects to /login. Registering lands the user directly
on the dashboard for their chosen role.

7. WORKFLOW — CUSTOMER
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7.1 THE PURCHASE JOURNEY — FIVE SCREENS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SCREEN 1 — /design/:id
    PREVIEW ONLY. There are deliberately NO colour/size pickers here.
    The buyer TICKS WHICH PRODUCTS they want the design on. It is a
    MULTI-SELECT. The "from" price updates live to the sum of
    (floor + margin) over everything ticked; with nothing ticked it shows the
    cheapest single product + margin.
    -> passes the selection onward as ?products=601,603

SCREEN 2 — /customize/:id   "Customize your order"
    Per selected product the buyer chooses:
        colour     restricted to what the DESIGNER enabled for that product
        size       same restriction
        quantity   clamped to 1..99
    The design is previewed on the product mockup at the DESIGNER's placement.
    Unknown/unsupported product ids in the query string are filtered out; if
    none survive, the first supported product is selected automatically.

    *** THE CUSTOMIZATION RULE ***
    A marketplace design CANNOT be moved, scaled or repositioned by the
    customer. The artwork is preserved exactly as the designer intended.
    `customizationAllowed` is hardcoded to false for every designer design at
    creation time. Only a customer's OWN uploaded artwork is freely placeable.
    The designer has no say in this — it is a platform-level rule.

    -> saves a CustomizationDraft (designId + items[] + selectedPrinterId null)

SCREEN 3 — /printers/:id   Printer selection
    Shows only printers that:
        (a) have availability === 'available', AND
        (b) offer EVERY product in the basket, AND
        (c) are not retired.
    Filters: rank, location, max delivery days (default 14).
    Sorts:   rating (default), rank, delivery time, or total price.
    Each card shows THAT printer's total for the WHOLE multi-product order,
    plus their own per-product notes.
    Landing here without a draft bounces back to /customize.

    -> "Send request" creates the order REQUESTED and goes straight to
       tracking. NO PAYMENT HAPPENS HERE.

SCREEN 4 — /tracking/:orderId
    Six-step timeline. While REQUESTED, a cancel action is available. Once
    ACCEPTED, a pay action appears.

SCREEN 5 — /cart or /checkout
    Lists every ACCEPTED + UNPAID order. Per order: choose payment method
    (D17 default / Paymee / card / cash), confirm or edit the shipping address
    (recipient, line, city, one of the 24 Tunisian governorates, phone), pay.
    The form pre-fills from the customer's default saved address, falling back
    to their profile address. Removing an order here cancels it.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7.2 PERSONAL PRINTING — THE CUSTOMER'S OWN ARTWORK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A parallel journey at /upload-design (customer and admin only — a designer
publishing work uses the dashboard wizard instead, and a printer cannot order
at all).

Upload PNG / JPEG / SVG / WebP, maximum 1 MB. The result is a Design that is:

    PRIVATE          never in the marketplace, never in a storefront, visible
                     only to its owner (and admins)
    AUTO-APPROVED    bypasses the moderation queue entirely
    ASSIGNED TO ALL  every product in the catalog is available to it
    FULLY CUSTOMIZABLE   the only artwork a buyer may reposition and rescale
    ROYALTY-FREE     designerRoyalty = 0; the platform keeps the full margin
    CATEGORY         "Custom"

From there it joins the normal flow at /customize.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7.3 CUSTOMER DASHBOARD (/dashboard) — tabs: Overview | My Orders | Settings
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Overview    total orders, delivered count, in-flight count, total spend.
My Orders   every order with its live stage badge; the review action appears
            once delivered.
Settings    profile fields (name, email, address)
            SAVED ADDRESSES — label, recipient, line1, city, governorate,
              postal code, phone, default flag. The FIRST address added
              automatically becomes the default; setting a new default clears
              the old one; removing the default promotes the next address.
            PAYMENT PREFERENCES — label, provider (card / D17 / cash),
              details, enabled flag, default flag; same default-promotion
              logic.
            favourite printers, free-text delivery notes.

/profile is a lighter view of the same: avatar URL, full name, email, primary
address, delivery notes.

8. REVIEWS AND RATINGS
================================================================================

  - A review is possible ONLY after the order reaches Delivered:
        "You can only review an order after it is delivered."
  - The customer rates TWO SEPARATE TARGETS for the same order:
        1. the DESIGN
        2. the PRINTER
    Each has its own star rating and comment. The dashboard modal collects
    both at once and submits them as two independent reviews (using the
    order's first line to resolve which design and which printer).
  - One review per (order, target):
        "You already reviewed the <design|printer> for this order."
  - A printer review also increments that pressroom's review counter.
  - Aggregation: new rating blended as (old + new) / 2, rounded to one
    decimal. See the note in 5.4(b).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9.2 PUBLISHING — THE TWO-STEP WIZARD (designer dashboard, Designs tab)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 1 — the artwork
    - upload the file: PNG / JPEG / SVG / WebP, MAX 1 MB
      (rejections: "Unsupported file type. Use PNG, JPEG, SVG or WebP." /
       "Image is too large (X MB). Maximum is 1 MB.")
    - title, description
    - CATEGORIES: multi-select from the ADMIN-CONTROLLED list
    - TAGS: free text, lowercased, de-duplicated, MAXIMUM 10
    - NO PRICE FIELD EXISTS.
    Validation: artwork required ("Please upload your artwork before
    continuing."), at least one category ("Pick at least one category.").

STEP 2 — product compatibility and per-product setup
    Tick every global product this design may be printed on. For each one:
        - placement: x, y, scale of the artwork on the mockup
        - which of the product's colours are allowed (defaults to ALL)
        - which of the product's sizes are allowed (defaults to ALL)
        - optional product-specific title and description
    Validation: at least one product ("Select at least one product this design
    can be printed on.").

PUBLISH -> the design is created with moderation = PENDING.
IT IS NOT LIVE. An admin must approve it before it appears anywhere public.

Editing an existing design reloads it into the same wizard, including every
per-product configuration. The storefront cards deep-link into this
(?edit=<designId>).

    The owning designer -> ALL their designs, and every card becomes an edit
                           shortcut, with a hint: "This is your storefront.
                           Click any design to edit..."

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9.5 DESIGNER RANKS — automatic, derived from salesScore
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Novice     score <  20     (starting level)
    Rising     score >= 20
    Artisan    score >= 60
    Elite      score >= 120

Recomputed at settlement and stamped onto BOTH the user record AND every one
of that designer's designs, so marketplace cards never show a stale rank.
An admin can also override a rank directly.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9.6 WHAT A DESIGNER CANNOT DO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - set or influence the customer price
  - choose which printer prints their work
  - create products or upload product mockups
  - create categories (admin owns that list)
  - let customers reposition their artwork
  - publish without admin approval

10. WORKFLOW — PRINTER (PRESSROOM)
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10.1 ONBOARDING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Register as printer -> a PrinterPartner (pressroom) record is created
alongside the user -> PENDING_VERIFICATION -> admin verifies -> ACTIVE and
selectable by buyers.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10.2 FULFILMENT SETUP (Account tab + /printer-profile)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    business name, contact email, contact phone, location, governorate,
    about text, coverage zones
    PRINTING METHODS (multi-select):
        DTF | sublimation | screen-printing | embroidery | vinyl
    processing days (typical time before shipping, default 3)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10.3 THE AVAILABILITY SYSTEM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

One toggle, three states:     available | busy | holiday

ONLY 'available' printers appear in printer selection.

This is the pressure valve of the entire marketplace: an overloaded or closed
shop removes itself from the pool instantly, which is what stops the
accept/reject step from becoming a bottleneck. A retired (demoted) pressroom
is forced to 'holiday'.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10.4 PRODUCT OPT-IN AND PRICING (Products tab)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The printer sees the ENTIRE global catalog and, per product:
    - tick to support it (creates an offering)
    - set their production price — the floor is displayed, and anything lower
      is silently raised to it with an explanatory message
    - write their own description for that product
    - toggle available / unavailable WITHOUT deleting the offering
    - untick to stop offering it entirely (deletes the offering)

Price and description are held as local drafts so typing does not commit on
every keystroke.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10.5 DASHBOARD TABS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Overview        products supported, revenue, fulfilment rate, rating.
Order requests  every REQUESTED order containing one of their lines, awaiting
                accept/reject. A separate list shows requests the CUSTOMER
                cancelled.
Print queue     ONLY accepted + PAID lines — a printer never starts work on an
                unpaid order. Each line advances one step at a time.
Products        the opt-in / pricing grid above.
Analytics       confirmed count, delivered count, distinct customer count,
                orders-by-status donut, product-mix donut (weighted by
                quantity).
Earnings        payout records, one per delivered order line.
Account         fulfilment setup and availability.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10.6 PRINTER RANKS — automatic, from fulfillmentScore (+5 per delivered order)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Verified   score <  50     (starting level for every verified shop)
    Gold       score >= 50
    Premium    score >= 120

Rank is both a filter and a sort key in printer selection, so it directly
drives how much work a printer wins. Admins can also set a rank manually.

Seeded pressrooms:
    501  PrintPro Tunisia     Premium   4.9  98% fulfilment  2 days  Tunis
    502  Atelier Couleurs     Gold      4.7  94% fulfilment  3 days  Sfax
    503  Studio Press Nabeul  Verified  4.5  90% fulfilment  4 days  Nabeul

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10.7 WHAT A PRINTER CANNOT DO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - BUY ANYTHING (hard rule, enforced in the router and again in the UI:
    "Printer accounts cannot place orders.")
  - create products or upload mockups
  - price below the admin floor
  - see or touch an order before the customer sends the request
  - start production before payment
  - un-reject or re-accept a decided request

11. WORKFLOW — ADMIN
================================================================================

Tabs: Overview | Users | Moderation | Catalog | Designs | Orders | Settings

OVERVIEW
    Total users, total orders, TOTAL REVENUE, active designs, active printers.
    Admin-only — never render this on a public page. The public homepage uses
    a separate, deliberately non-sensitive stat set.

USERS
    Search across name, email and role. Per user:
      - VERIFY (PENDING_VERIFICATION -> ACTIVE)
      - SUSPEND / BAN / REACTIVATE (both suspension and ban force an immediate
        logout of that user's session)
      - reassign role — assignable roles are customer, designer, printer.
        NOBODY CAN BE PROMOTED TO ADMIN through the UI.
      - override designer rank or printer rank

MODERATION — THE CONTENT GATE
    Every new designer design lands in a PENDING queue.
      APPROVED -> becomes visible in the marketplace
      REJECTED -> design status forced to REMOVED, permanently off-platform
    The designer is notified either way:
      "Your design "<title>" was approved and is now live." / "...rejected."
    Every decision is appended to an immutable moderation log (admin id,
    target type, target id, action, reason, timestamp).
    Designs also carry an `nsfw` flag in the model.
    Customer personal uploads bypass this queue entirely.

CATALOG — THE ADMIN OWNS THE PRODUCTS
    Create / edit / delete global products: name, category, description,
    basePrice (THE FLOOR EVERY PRINTER MUST RESPECT), colours (comma list),
    sizes (comma list), mockup images (one URL per line — several supported),
    availability (ACTIVE / PAUSED / DRAFT), lead time days.
    Deleting a product CASCADES: every printer offering for it is removed, and
    it is stripped from every design's assigned-product list.

DESIGNS AND FEATURED CONTENT
    Force any design to ACTIVE / ARCHIVED / REMOVED.
    Feature or unfeature a DESIGN, a DESIGNER or a PRINTER — the curation
    lever. Featuring is a toggle and is also written to the moderation log.

ORDERS
    Read-only oversight of every order on the platform with its live stage.

SETTINGS — THE ECONOMIC CONTROLS
    Three numbers that reprice the whole marketplace instantly:
        margin | designerRoyalty | payoutThreshold
    Plus THE ONE marketplace category list, edited as a comma-separated field.
    This single list drives the marketplace filter, the designer upload
    wizard AND the homepage mood tiles. At least one must remain:
        "Keep at least one category."

12. ACCOUNTS AND VERIFICATION
================================================================================

REGISTRATION
    Fields: name, email, address, password, role.
    Self-selectable roles: customer, designer, printer. Never admin.
    Duplicate email is rejected: "An account with this email already exists."

    Status on creation:
        customer  -> ACTIVE                (shops immediately)
        designer  -> PENDING_VERIFICATION
        printer   -> PENDING_VERIFICATION  (+ a pressroom record is created)

ACCOUNT LIFECYCLE
    AccountStatus = ACTIVE | PENDING_VERIFICATION | SUSPENDED | BANNED

    Pending designers/printers are redirected to /verification-pending on
    every guarded route. Verified users cannot reach that page.

    The login gate is applied on BOTH the API path and the local path, so a
    blocked account can never obtain a session:
        SUSPENDED -> "This account is suspended. Contact Printymand support."
        BANNED    -> "This account has been banned for policy violations."
        bad creds -> "Invalid email or password."

    Status-change notifications:
        ACTIVE               "Your account has been verified and is now
                              active."
        PENDING_VERIFICATION "Your account is pending verification."
        SUSPENDED            "Your account has been suspended."
        BANNED               "Your account has been banned for policy
                              violations."

ROLE CHANGES
    An admin can move a user between customer / designer / printer.
    Demoting a printer RETIRES the pressroom (retired = true, availability
    forced to 'holiday') rather than deleting it — historical order lines and
    offerings keep resolving, and it is excluded from printer selection and
    from public pressroom counts. Re-promoting un-retires the same record.

PROFILE EDITS PROPAGATE
    Changing a user's name/avatar cascades to: their credential record (on
    email change), their pressroom's business name, and the denormalised
    designer name and avatar stored on every one of their designs.

13. NOTIFICATIONS
================================================================================

Generated at every meaningful transition. Types:

    order_requested    request sent / received
    order_accepted     accepted -> "go to your cart to pay"
    order_rejected     rejected by printer, or cancelled by customer
    order_paid         payment received -> "start fulfillment"
    order_status       EVERY fulfilment step change
    design_moderated   approved and live, or rejected
    account_status     verified / pending / suspended / banned
    payout             payout requested, royalties earned

Each carries a deep link (/tracking/<id>, /cart, /printer-dashboard,
/designer-dashboard). /notifications is open to any signed-in role, shows
newest first, marks a notification read on click, and offers mark-all-as-read.
Unread items are visually flagged with a "New" pill.


================================================================================
14. THE BUSINESS INVARIANTS
================================================================================

The rules the system will not let you break. A backend must enforce every one
of these server-side — the frontend currently enforces them alone.

     1. No payment before printer acceptance.
     2. No production before payment.
     3. Printers never buy.
     4. Designers never price.
     5. Printers never price below the admin floor.
     6. Only the admin creates products.
     7. Only the admin creates design categories.
     8. No designer artwork reaches the public without admin approval.
     9. Marketplace artwork is never repositioned by the buyer; only the
        buyer's own uploads are placeable.
    10. A customer's personal upload never enters the marketplace and never
        earns a royalty.
    11. Only 'available', non-retired printers offering EVERY product in the
        basket can be selected.
    12. Reviews only after delivery, once per order per target.
    13. Royalties and printer payouts release only when an order is FULLY
        delivered, and only once.
    14. Ranks are earned automatically from scores (admin override aside).
    15. Suspended and banned accounts cannot hold a session.
    16. Removing the printer role retires the pressroom rather than deleting
        it, so order history never breaks.
    17. The design<->product link is stored on ONE side only.
    18. Public pages never expose revenue or total user count.

================================================================================
16. HOW STATE WORKS TODAY, AND WHERE A BACKEND PLUGS IN
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
16.1 TODAY: ONE SIGNAL STORE, PERSISTED TO THE BROWSER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

All of the above runs inside PlatformStoreService — a single
signal<PlatformState> holding users, credentials, printers, products,
offerings, designs, cart items, orders, reviews, payouts, printer payouts,
notifications, featured content, moderation log, customization draft and
platform settings.

  - It persists to localStorage on every change, under the versioned key
    `printymand_platform_state_v7`.
  - On load, persisted data is merged OVER the seed data by id — persisted
    entries always win, and seed entries the browser has never seen are
    appended. So new seed products appear without wiping the user's own work.
  - `nextId` is reconciled against the highest id in use, so ids cannot be
    reissued.
  - Writes can fail (uploaded artwork is inlined as base64 data URLs and
    blows the ~5 MB quota). The failure is surfaced, not swallowed:
      "Local storage is full — recent changes are kept for this session only.
       Remove some uploaded designs to save again."

CREDENTIALS ARE NOT SECURITY. Local passwords are stored as an FNV-1a digest,
explicitly documented in the code as NOT a security control — it only avoids
readable passwords in localStorage. Real authentication is a backend concern.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
16.2 THE BRIDGE ALREADY BUILT FOR YOUR BACKEND
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

`environment.useRealApi` decides whether HTTP is attempted at all:

    false (dev)   pure local store            backendMode = 'mock'
    true  (prod)  every mutation mirrors to the API; if a call fails the app
                  keeps working locally       backendMode = 'hybrid'
                  when calls succeed          backendMode = 'api'

Two helpers wrap this:
    tryApi()    awaits the call, returns null when the API is off or failed
                (telling the caller to fall back to local state)
    syncApi()   fires the call without blocking the UI

`services/api.service.ts` is already written as the FULL INTENDED REST
CONTRACT — catalog reads, cart, orders, auth, reviews, the whole order
lifecycle (submit / accept / reject / cancel / pay / advance line), profiles,
printer products, designs and moderation, payouts, notifications and admin.
Base URL /api/v1. Credentials are attached by httpCredentialsInterceptor.

That file is the endpoint list to build against.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
16.3 WHAT THE FRONTEND CANNOT ENFORCE, AND YOUR BACKEND MUST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    - PRICES MUST BE RECOMPUTED SERVER-SIDE. The client freezes
      printerAmount / platformFee / designerRoyalty onto each line. A client
      that sends those values is an attack vector.
    - SETTLEMENT NEEDS REAL IDEMPOTENCY. Today it is guarded by a
      read-then-check on existing payout rows; in a database that wants a
      transaction plus a unique constraint.
    - CONCURRENCY DOES NOT EXIST HERE. One user, one tab: two printers cannot
      race, a printer cannot double-accept, ids cannot collide. All of that
      becomes real server-side.
    - IDs: the frontend counts integers upward from 10000. The order
      reference PMD-YYMMDD-<seq> needs a server-side sequence.
    - EVERY INVARIANT IN SECTION 14 is currently enforced only in the browser.
